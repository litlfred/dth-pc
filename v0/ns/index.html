KG concepts are written Capitalized (owner, 2026-10-31). Supply chain terms (traceability, the foundational components, order and warehouse management) are held in dth-sc, which dth-pc depends on; general digital terms (registry, interoperability, open standards, …) in smart-ra, with this handbook's wording as a cited alternative (litlfred/smart-ra#142). Every term is a candidate: only a person adopts a definition. When the smart-ra and dth-sc schemes are mounted, each term gains closeMatch/broadMatch links to the concept it narrows. Scope: the handbook does not cover medical devices (page 16); nothing here extends to them (owner, 2026-10-31, workplan Q4). Acronyms (owner, 2026-10-31): every acronym the handbook uses is a term here with the acronym as notation, unless smart-ra or dth-sc already holds it (FHIR, DHSC, WMS). Organizations several handbooks name live once in smart-ra's organizations scheme and are listed under members; organizations only this handbook names (Rwanda FDA, NAFDAC, ZAMRA, ZAMMSA, RMS, GHSC-PSM) are terms here with a foaf:focus to a W3C ORG organization (litlfred/folio-assistant-core#411). more
