Definition
Product master data consists of standardized information that accurately describes a health product’s characteristics. This data remains consistent across all instances of a product. (en)
Scope note
Handbook wording, quoted. A candidate: not adopted, nothing chosen between alternatives. Examples the handbook gives: product description, dosage, pharmaceutical strength, manufacturer details, shelf life. Owner, 2026-10-31: one concept, no homonym split with the RA's 'Product'. Not defined in dth-sc; this handbook is its source.
