**Lanes (Roles):**

- **National Regulatory Authority (PC-R-06)** (`PC-R-06`): Lead regulatory organization (p.21-23)
- **Ministry of Health (PC-R-08)** (`PC-R-08`): Collaborating health authority and policy stakeholder (p.21-22)
- **Manufacturer / Distributor (PC-R-07)** (`PC-R-07`): Source of truth for Product Master Data; consulted on phased roll-out (p.21-23)
- **Master Data Governance Group (PC-R-04)** (`PC-R-04`): Multi-stakeholder governance body supporting policy review and compliance (p.22-23)

**Process Steps:**

1. **Step 1: Identify necessary regulatory policies and legislations** — Ch 1 Step 1 (p.21): Analyse current regulations for Product Master Data standardization and sharing; identify required additional policies and updates.
2. **Step 2: Develop the identified regulatory policies and legislations** — Ch 1 Step 2 (p.22): Develop master data guidelines and product identification/labelling policies consistent with data standardization and AIDC; outline phased roll-out timelines.
3. **Step 3: Coordinate reviews and approvals of the drafted policies** — Ch 1 Step 3 (p.22): Evaluate newly developed and revised policies led by NRA in collaboration with MoH, warehouses, procurement entities, manufacturers/distributors, and governance stakeholders; incorporate feedback and obtain approvals.
4. **Step 4: Publish and enforce approved regulatory policies** — Ch 1 Step 4 (p.23): Publish approved policy document and define mechanisms to institutionalize and monitor compliance by manufacturers and distributors.

**Inputs and Outputs (Data Objects):**

- Existing regulatory policies and legislations (Input, p.21)
- List of additional regulatory policies and modifications required (Output Step 1 / Input Step 2, p.21-22)
- Master data related policies and legislations draft (Output Step 2 / Input Step 3, p.22)
- Approved and endorsed master data policies and legislations (Output Step 3-4, p.22-23)
