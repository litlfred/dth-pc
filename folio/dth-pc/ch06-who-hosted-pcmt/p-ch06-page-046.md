Countries planning to implement a digital tool to manage product master data may consider using the WHO– hosted PCMT as an initial option. This tool can serve as a starting point to manage national health product master data while countries build the necessary technical resources and capacity to implement an in-country– hosted solution. Alternatively, countries may continue using the WHO-hosted PCMT if it meets their ongoing needs. The WHO-hosted PCMT will be available in 2026.
