Why is Product Master Data important? Product master data is central to all operational processes across supply chain partners. It ensures that each entity uniformly understands the transactional information exchanged throughout the supply chain. Acting as a common language, product master data links all supply chain data—including orders, shipments, and inventory— from manufacturing to dispensing. This standardization helps supply chain partners, processes, and systems interoperate effectively. Similarly, facility master data plays a critical role in enabling national supply chain partners to coordinate the delivery of health products to the correct locations.
