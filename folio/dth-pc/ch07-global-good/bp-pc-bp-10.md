**Process ID:** `PC-BP-10` | **BPMN 2.0 Diagram:** `processes/PC-BP-10.bpmn` | **Source:** `who-iris/library/9789240116191-eng` (page-049, page-050)

**Lanes (Roles):**

- **Country User Group (PC-R-09: WHO-hosted PCMT or in-country NPC tool)** (`PC-R-09`): Country sharing national Product Master Data with the WHO Global Product Catalogue (p.49-50, Fig. 15)
- **WHO User Group (PC-R-09: WHO Global Product Catalogue)** (`PC-R-09`): Aggregates, harmonizes, and publishes shared Product Master Data in the Global Catalogue as a global good (p.49-50, Fig. 15)

**Process Steps:**

1. **Submit national Product Master Data to WHO Global Catalogue** — Ch 7 (p.49-50, Fig. 15): Country (using either the WHO-hosted PCMT instance or an in-country NPC tool) shares standardized Product Master Data with the WHO Global Product Catalogue.
2. **Aggregate and harmonize shared Product Master Data across sources** — Ch 7 (p.49-50, Fig. 15): WHO Global Catalogue aggregates Product Master Data from multiple countries and sources and harmonizes differences to support global standardization.
3. **Publish harmonized Product Master Data in Global Catalogue for reuse by countries** — Ch 7 (p.49-50, Fig. 15): Publish updated Global Catalogue records so participating and other countries can access accurate product information and reduce duplication.

**Inputs and Outputs (Data Objects):**

- Shared national Product Master Data submission (Fig. 15, p.49-50)
- Harmonized WHO Global Product Catalogue dataset (Fig. 15, p.49-50)
