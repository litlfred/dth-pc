For effective use of data in operations, analysis, and decision-making, the integrity and quality of data must be consistently and continuously maintained. Establishing data management policies to maintain master data structures, definitions, and operational procedures is crucial for sustaining data integrity and quality. Data management policies should ensure adherence to principles of privacy, security, and ethical data usage. This chapter outlines steps to establish comprehensive data management policies and procedures. It is important to note that the steps might not always follow a linear order and may be implemented simultaneously or concurrently.
