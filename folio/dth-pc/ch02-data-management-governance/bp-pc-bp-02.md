**Lanes (Roles):**

- **Data Owner (PC-R-01)** (`PC-R-01`): Overall strategy, governance, and quality of Product Master Data (p.26)
- **Data Steward (PC-R-03)** (`PC-R-03`): Day-to-day management, maintenance, harmonization, and quality control (p.26-27)
- **Data Custodian (PC-R-02)** (`PC-R-02`): Technical management, security protocols, and access controls (p.26, 31-32)
- **Master Data Governance Group (PC-R-04)** (`PC-R-04`): Stakeholder governance group providing leadership and oversight (p.25, 32)

**Process Steps:**

1. **Step 1: Define Product Master Data management procedures** — Ch 2 Step 1 (p.25-26): Review current product coding schemes and datasets; evaluate harmonization needs; develop master data management procedures and TOR for the Master Data Governance Group.
2. **Step 2: Define coding scheme, data definitions and structures** — Ch 2 Step 2 (p.27-30): Conduct data harmonization; establish standardized coding schemes and structures for Product Classification, Generic Product, Trade Item, Regulated Trade Item, and Packaging Hierarchy; map to HL7 FHIR, ISO IDMP, WHODrug, and GS1 GTIN.
3. **Step 3: Define data use policies and data sharing mechanisms** — Ch 2 Step 3 (p.31): Identify all master data sources and consumers; define data use policies, data sharing agreements, exchange mechanisms, and security/sensitivity protocols.
4. **Step 4: Institutionalise Product Master Data governance policies and procedures** — Ch 2 Step 4 (p.32): Establish the Master Data Governance Group and integrate master data management procedures, coding schemes, and governance policies across health supply chain operations.
5. **Step 5: Define short- and long-term strategies to source standardized Product Master Data** — Ch 2 Step 5 (p.33): Define short/medium-term sourcing (procurement agencies, donors, product packaging) and long-term sustainable sourcing (formal submission during regulatory product registration).

**Inputs and Outputs (Data Objects):**

- Endorsed master data policies and legislations (Input from Ch 1, p.25, 33)
- Master data management procedures and TOR for Master Data Governance Group (Output Step 1, p.25)
- Standardized master data coding schemes and structures (Output Step 2, p.27)
- Data sharing and data use policies (Output Step 3, p.31)
- Short-term and long-term strategies for sourcing master data (Output Step 5, p.33)
