The effectiveness of processes and their supporting systems depends on the availability of human resources with the appropriate skill sets to operate the systems and execute the processes. In addition to technical skills, the workforce must also understand the purpose of improved systems and processes, along with the benefits they bring to their daily work. Human resource capacity is a critical component for the successful implementation and adoption of global standards–based master data management systems and processes. This chapter outlines the steps that countries can follow to build human resource capacity to sustainably manage the NPC—including its system, processes, and data.
