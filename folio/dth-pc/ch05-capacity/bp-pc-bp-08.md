> **Unassigned Lane Flag:** FLAGGED FOR OWNER: Chapter 5 (pages 43-44) names Catalogue Manager (PC-R-05) and NPC System Administrator (PC-R-18) as target operational/technical roles, but names no performer for HR planning, budgeting, recruitment, and knowledge management; those tasks are placed in an explicit unassigned lane per workplan §2e.

**Lanes (Roles):**

- **Unassigned (flagged: Ch 5 p.43-44 names no performer for HR planning, budgeting, recruitment, and KM)** (`unassigned`): FLAGGED FOR OWNER: Chapter 5 does not name the performing role for HR capacity planning, budgeting, recruitment, and knowledge management.
- **Catalogue Manager (PC-R-05)** (`PC-R-05`): Named operational role onboarded and assigned to manage the NPC and Product Master Data processes (p.43-44)

**Process Steps:**

1. **Step 1: Identify human resource capacity needs to support NPC** — Ch 5 Step 1 (p.43): Determine HR capacity required; identify operational and technical roles (e.g., Catalogue Manager, NPC System Administrator); define job descriptions and update the HR management plan.
2. **Step 2a: Develop budgets, identify funding, and recruit personnel** — Ch 5 Step 2 (p.44): Develop budgets, identify funding sources, and recruit and train personnel with required skill sets to fill the identified roles.
3. **Step 2b: Onboard and execute assigned NPC operational responsibilities** — Ch 5 Step 2 (p.43-44): Onboarded Catalogue Manager (and technical staff) assume operational responsibility for the NPC according to defined job descriptions and SOPs.
4. **Step 3: Develop knowledge management plan and local capacity strategy** — Ch 5 Step 3 (p.44): Define organogram, knowledge management plan for master data and NPC, and governmental strategy to engage and enable local vendors and technical capacity.

**Inputs and Outputs (Data Objects):**

- List of roles and job descriptions to support NPC (Output Step 1, p.43-44)
- Human resource management plan and budget (Output Step 1-2, p.43-44)
- Onboarded resources and Organogram (Output Step 2-3, p.44)
- Knowledge management plan and governmental strategy for local technical capacity (Output Step 3, p.44)
